"Steph Curry is the greatest shooter God ever created."That's what I've always said. Here's the data.

For every shot, an XGBoost model estimates what an average NBA shooter that season would score from the same spot and game situation. Curry's points above expected is what he scored on top of that. Pick seasons, filter the shots, and click a bar in the season chart to jump to that year.

Why the model ignores NBA.com's shot labels. Before 2017-18, labels like "Pullup Jump Shot" were mostly written down when the shot went in: in 2009-10, threes labelled "pull-up" went in 71% of the time against a 35.5% league average. A model using them is partly told the answer, so this one uses only location, game clock and season.
